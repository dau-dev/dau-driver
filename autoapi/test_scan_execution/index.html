test_scan_execution¶

The streaming scan runner against the in-memory FakeTransport: the ping-pong protocol (region alternation, exact INPUT_LENGTH, padded DMA), the overlap guarantee (a job only completes once the next batch’s DMA landed), and the error paths.

test_scan_execution.test_batch_balance_is_bounded_by_the_largest_key() → None¶

A key cannot be split without resetting the per-key state the partitioning exists to protect, so one dominant key caps how evenly batches can balance — and therefore how much overlap can hide. Pinned because the balancing heuristic could otherwise be read as a promise it cannot keep under skew.

test_scan_execution.test_a_resident_run_uploads_nothing(tmp_path) → None¶

The whole point: rows already on the device are queried without a DMA. On the measured platform the feed is the larger term of the device path (40.0 ms total against a ~10.4 ms job), so removing it from the loop is what amortization means.

test_scan_execution.test_a_resident_run_still_programs_the_exact_input_length(tmp_path) → None¶

Address and length are the contract; getting either wrong reads the wrong bytes and would be indistinguishable from a bad answer.

test_scan_execution.test_a_misaligned_resident_address_is_refused(tmp_path) → None¶

An unaligned DMA region is a silent-wrong-bytes failure on silicon, so it is refused where the mistake is still cheap.
